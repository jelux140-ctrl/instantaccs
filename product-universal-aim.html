About Universal Aim

Universal Aim is an AI-driven aim assist. Instead of reading game memory, it watches the screen in real time and uses a trained vision model to find targets – which is why it runs on virtually any title without a game-specific build. Tune the FOV, smoothing and confidence to taste, and it behaves the same whether you are playing a shooter it has seen before or one it has not.
